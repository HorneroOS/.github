# Code of Conduct

HorneroOS spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions — are
open to everyone. To keep them welcoming and productive:

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing.
- Assume good intent; focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, hate speech or personal attacks.
- No spam, advertising or off-topic content.
- Respect the privacy of others; do not publish personal data.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when these
expectations are not met.

## Reporting

To report a violation, open an issue in
[HorneroOS/.github](https://github.com/HorneroOS/.github/issues) marked as
confidential where possible, or contact the organization owners through the
[HorneroOS people page](https://github.com/orgs/HorneroOS/people). Reports are
handled discreetly.
